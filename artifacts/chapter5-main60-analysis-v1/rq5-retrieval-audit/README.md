# RQ5 Section 5.6.3 retrieval audit

This offline artifact reconstructs all 720 independent E1--E6 retrieval paths. R2 and R3 actions are replayed against the frozen local indexes with their recorded query terms, date filters, and seen-state. Gold annotations define diagnostic funnel coordinates; they do not automatically establish a causal retrieval failure. A native cap hit is reported as a process state, never as an automatic failure. Only retriever reach and model-observation fields are updated in the 250-case state register; downstream evidence-retention, sufficiency, Answerer, fusion, long-context, and evaluation states are unchanged. The observation state means exposure of official annotated evidence, not decisive or sufficient evidence. A ranked search with no newly observed annotated locator may still be semantically useful. Likewise, a nonempty bundle without an annotated locator is not labelled irrelevant. Condition and category summaries freeze these descriptive diagnostics explicitly.
